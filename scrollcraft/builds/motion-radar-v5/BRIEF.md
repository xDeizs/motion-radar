# Motion Radar v5

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation. The user requests a rebuild through Scroll Craft, similar scroll animations, model-generated imagery, a beautiful background video or Google Flow prompts, clickable icons, and only the most necessary additional skills. This is a rebuild of an existing functioning free library, not a new marketing product. No new approval interview is required to keep this request moving.

## Evidence and creative decisions

1. **Vibe.** User requests: «Пересобери сайт еще раз через https://github.com/nateherkai/scroll-craft ... подобные должны быть анимации на сайте при скролле, генерируй изображения через свою модель ... красивые кликабельные иконки». Authored: dimensional, tactile, precise, curious. Preserve the existing lavender identity, with graphite and cool silver. No decorative neon or gradient text.
2. **Journey.** Authored: a short cinematic encounter with a material sculpture; a clear visual handoff from motion into useful destinations; the actual searchable collection; a quiet source/status ledger; a real saved collection or export action. All current tabs remain functional.
3. **Energy.** Authored: composed opening, one dimensional opening-to-midpoint peak, fast practical handoff, quiet reading, resolved useful close. Do not gate the library behind a long scroll story.
4. **Feel.** See the curve below. The one remembered moment is a floating material ribbon moving behind type as the scattered destination icons settle into a useful launch strip.
5. **Signature.** Authored: "Signal dock". Destination buttons begin around the sculpture and resolve into a labeled launch strip as scroll advances; choosing a destination opens the actual corresponding library tab and the chosen state carries into the real view. Buttons remain usable without motion and by keyboard. No fake telemetry.
6. **Range.** Authored: editorial/industrial premium, not maximalist. Real HTML text, cool silver materials, deliberate lavender action surfaces, strong spacing. A useful library remains the product.
7. **World/scene.** Distinct scenes. No unbroken journey and no chain of generated shots. A bespoke "library prologue" grammar is defined below.
8. **Assets.** Existing original AI illustrations and reference research, original code, seven RSS feeds, repository/resource catalog, notes, and lab. New imagery is generated with the authorized in-session image model. Kie credentials are absent and unnecessary; no Kie calls or paid runtime APIs. A short silent 2.5D motion-design video from generated assets is not represented as native generative video. Optional image-to-video prompts for Google Flow are supplied separately.

## Visitor / belief / action

For motion designers and creative coders. The visitor should believe: "I can find a real source, try an idea, and keep what is useful without another subscription." Primary action: «Открыть библиотеку». The lab is a distinct secondary destination, not a second label for the same action.

## Feeling curve (written before score)

- Curiosity: a complete quiet dark composition, concise headline, a highly specific silver/lavender material study.
- Delight (peak): the sculpture and independent near-plane separate while destination symbols find useful positions. The camera move is controlled by the hand, not arbitrary endless animation.
- Agency: the icons are real buttons that take the visitor directly to the selected working view.
- Focus: real source cards, filters, details, notes, and usable lab previews on calm surfaces.
- Trust: actual snapshot time and actual source/record counts, no invented performance claims.
- Resolve: saved/exported material, clear provenance, and a useful last screen that never fades to blank.

## Peak / tell-someone

Peak: "The sculpture moved behind the words, then the floating symbols became the tools I could actually open." It lives in the prologue. The prologue has the only long pinned span, 2.8 viewport heights desktop, 2.25 phone; every later story block is ordinary flow.

It's the site where the floating motion experiment turns into your working library.

## Grammar: Library prologue (bespoke, not a renamed filmic one-shot)

- Navigation: working library tabs plus an in-hero labeled destination dock. Multiple entry points are required.
- Sequence: one short pinned spatial prologue; an in-flow labeled handoff; the real dynamic library; a source ledger; a compact export/collection close. The working collection is not converted into pinned marketing acts.
- Ending: actual collection/export controls with source provenance, not a spotlight CTA or a filmic fade.
- Bans: a continuous background for the whole site, a second pinned type act, forced sideways rails, a footer that fades out, invented stats, counters for act numbers, scroll nudges, magnetic navigation, hidden essential copy, marketing claims baked into images, or overlaid text on the working card previews.
- Why the eight stock grammars lost: filmic one-shot forbids the navigation the library needs; chaptered editorial forbids this commissioned dimensional opening; live surface forbids the cinematic claim/media treatment requested; continuous world creates needless seams/weight; typographic poster rejects the requested imagery; catalog forbids a separate prologue; split stage has no honest two-sided argument here; cutlist forbids the requested held spatial scroll experience.

## Fingerprint gate

The user's registry was freshly seeded and empty. First Scroll Craft build has no previous row to compare. The prior Motion Radar v4 is not silently relabeled as a Scroll Craft build; v5 replaces its static hero and entry-card grid, preserving working product features.

## Layer contract

| Plane | Asset | Independent movement | Contract |
|---|---|---|---|
| Far | dark material-video plate/poster | slow camera drift / scrub | no duplicate foreground subject; full poster on failure |
| Mid | generated silver/lavender material sculpture, genuine alpha | restrained scale, translation, slight 2D angle | ungrounded intentional suspension; full silhouette retained |
| Near | separately generated silver ribbon detail, genuine alpha | opposite restrained displacement | frames image side, never occludes essential text/actions |
| Atmosphere | optional image-generated reflections in the background plate | slower than mid | no full-frame wash or neon halo |
| Type | real h1 and description | controlled prologue transition | fully readable at arrival; stays in own text column |
| Agency | semantic icon buttons and their labels | scroll resolves positions; focus resolves instantly | clickable at all positions, 44px+ targets |

Desktop: type on left, subject in right-side image field; no headline smeared over bright material. Phone: short two-line type above a separate image field; controls below/alongside the image, no shrunken desktop overlap. On wide screens (1280px+) the five buttons follow ordered, non-crossing trajectories. On phones and medium screens the destination dock stays in a stable bottom row instead of shrinking the desktop arrangement. Reduced motion: complete static composition, no pin tax and no video fetch.

## Score (after curve / grammar)

| Beat | Device family | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Dimensional prologue / peak | scrub + layered parallax | a real material camera loop under the scroll; layers visibly separate |
| Library handoff | reveal | the launch strip resolves into actual destinations |
| Working collection | flow + in | reading and using, no scrolling tax |
| Source ledger | count (real values only) | real snapshot evidence with labels |
| Useful close | flow / static hold | export, collection and source provenance stay reachable |

Four distinct families: scrub, parallax, reveal, flow/in, plus optional count. No device repeated as adjacent story acts. No authored dead scroll or decorative silence. Controls remain real throughout.
